Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Data controller

The data controller is Muhtar Ağcabay (an individual), who publishes the Tavla app as Fernwood Studio. Contact: fernwood.studio.contact@gmail.com. This policy explains which personal data the Tavla app processes, why and how, under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

Data we process

• Account: a random account ID; your Google account’s email address if you connect Google.

• Profile: display name, chosen avatar, uploaded profile photo, country and (in Turkey) province.

• Game: your online matches (moves, dice, score, date), rating, badges and statistics.

• Credits and store: credit balance and history, cosmetics you bought and equipped.

• Social: friendships and requests, players you blocked, reports you made and reports about you.

• Device: a notification token if you allow notifications; language preference.

Offline games and settings stay on your device and are not sent to the server.

Purposes and legal grounds

• Creating your account and providing online play, matchmaking, rating, friends and the store (performance of a contract).

• Preventing cheating, harassment and inappropriate content: automatic photo moderation, reports and blocking (legitimate interest).

• Sending your-turn and game invite notifications (only if you allow them; you can turn them off in Settings at any time).

• Meeting legal obligations.

Where data is stored and sharing

Data is stored on a server (VDS) rented by the data controller; photo moderation also runs on this server. Data is not sold or shared for advertising.

Limited data is transferred to providers abroad only for these services:

• Google sign-in: authentication is done by Google.

• Notifications: your notification token and the notification content (e.g. “Your turn”) are delivered through Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.

Your display name, avatar, photo, country, rating and featured badges are visible to other players in matches and on your profile.

Retention

Data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted immediately; copies in server backups are permanently deleted within 30 days as backups rotate.

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app under Settings > Profile > Delete account. If you cannot access the app, email fernwood.studio.contact@gmail.com with your display name and (if any) your Google email address; the request is completed within 30 days.

Deleted: your profile (display name, avatar, photo, country, province), credit balance and history, store purchases, badges, friendships, blocks, reports and notification registrations.

Kept: matches you played stay in your opponents’ match history without your name or any link to your account. Remaining credits and purchases are not refunded.

Your rights

You can ask whether your data is processed, request information about it, learn its purpose and who it is shared with, request correction or deletion, object to an adverse result of automated analysis and claim compensation. Send requests to fernwood.studio.contact@gmail.com; they are answered within 30 days. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority.

Children

The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data; such accounts are deleted when discovered. Users aged 13–18 should use the app with the permission of a parent or guardian.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published in the app and on the web page; the last updated date is shown at the top.
